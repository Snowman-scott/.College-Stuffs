## Considering user needs

### Visual impairment
*Your user has difficulty seeing parts of the interface and may use a screen reader.*

1. **What parts of the product might be difficult for your user?**  
    Navigating the menus
2. **Why might they struggle?**  
  The Menus are packed full of tabs which may be hard for a user to scroll across and have a screen reader read.
3. **What could the designer change?**  
   1. Menu spacing
   2. Larger text in the menus 
   3. Bigger / better menu icons

### Dyslexia
*Your user can find dense text, unusual layouts and some typography choices difficult to process.*

1. **What parts of the product might be difficult for your user?**  
   Large blocks of text and info in one place
2. **Why might they struggle?**  
   It is hard to read and process a lot of information and data that is all cramped up in one place.
3. **What could the designer change?**  
   1. Spread out the information across more pages
   2. Split up the text across the page more
   3. add an optional dyslexia friendly font face toggle

---

### ADHD
*Your user can find distracting interfaces, complex navigation and information overload difficult to manage.*

1. **What parts of the product might be difficult for your user?**  
    General navigation
2. **Why might they struggle?**  
   There is a lot of buttons (links) to press and the sections sometimes are not labeled properly
3. **What could the designer change?**  
   1. Improve the section names
   2. improve upon the homepage 
   3. make the icons more specific and helpful
