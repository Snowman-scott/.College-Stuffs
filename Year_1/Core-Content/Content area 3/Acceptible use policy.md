1.	A AUP is a set of rules and guidance for using organisations systems, networks and digital resources
2.	 To protect infrastructure from misuse and damage, Reduce risks of breaches and unauthorised access
3.	One ethical principle is privacy, this is to keep your personal information safe and with only you. For example you don’t access files or mail without permission even if you have the opportunity to
Another ethical principle is security, This is to keep you and what you do online private and safe. An example is using encryption and strong authentication for sensitive data
Finally Accuracy and honesty. Ensuring all information is truthful and not misleading. For example avoid fake accounts, misinformation and data manipulation
