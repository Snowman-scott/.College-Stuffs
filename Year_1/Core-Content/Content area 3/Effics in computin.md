Gamergate
Gamergate was an online Harassment campaign that began in 2014. This campaign targeted Woman in the gaming industry under the guise of advocating for “Ethics in gaming journalism” 
Gamergate Originated from websites like 4chan, reddit and 8chan before spreading to twitter via the #Gamergate hashtag.
This falls into the Harassment/ Sexual harassment category of issues as it is harassing woman working innocently in he games industry for no reason what so ever.
They targeted three main people:
1.	Zoe Quinn – Game dev who faced the first wave of harassment
2.	Anita Sarkeesian – Feminist media critic who analysed sexism in video games
3.	Brianna Wu – Game dev and Critic
There were real world impacts because of this campaign For example:
1.	Physical safety threats arose around Quinn. Quinn became the focus of online sexual harassment, These included threats of Rape and Death, Many other Anonymous gamers made Threats like this to Anita and Brianna as well 
2.	Forced displacement. After an episode of Tropes vs. Woman aired on 24/08/14 Anita Received numerous death and rape threats, as well as getting private information like her home address leaked
3.	Events getting cancelled. Due to this campaign Anita was forced to cancel a speech at Utah college due to a threat against her Life. Someone claiming to be a student emailed staff members saying that they would commit “The deadliest school shooting in American History” if the event was not cancelled
