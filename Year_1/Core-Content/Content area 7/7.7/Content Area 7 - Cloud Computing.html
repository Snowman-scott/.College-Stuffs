What is Cloud Computing?

Definition

Cloud computing is the on-demand delivery of processing power, database, storage, applications, and other IT resources via the Internet with pay-as-you-go pricing.

☁️
Slide 5 / 20

Infrastructure as Software

Cloud computing enables you to stop thinking of your infrastructure as hardware, and instead think of (and use) it as software.

🏢

Hardware

Physical servers, data centres, equipment that require space, power, and maintenance

💻

Software

Flexible, programmable, scalable resources accessible via APIs and management consoles

Slide 6 / 20

Cloud Service Models

🔧

IaaS

Infrastructure as a Service

Basic building blocks: compute, storage, networking

⚙️

PaaS

Platform as a Service

Focus on deployment and management of applications

📱

SaaS

Software as a Service

Complete product run and managed by service provider

Slide 7 / 20

Cloud Deployment Models

☁️

Cloud

Fully deployed in the cloud, all parts of the application run in the cloud

🔀

Hybrid

Connect cloud-based resources to on-premises infrastructure

🏢

On-Premises

Deploy resources using virtualization and resource management tools

Slide 10 / 20

Trade Capital Expense for Variable Expense

Traditional IT

  • Large upfront investment
  • Depreciation over time
  • Risk of over-provisioning
  • Risk of under-provisioning

Cloud Computing

  • Pay only for what you use
  • No upfront costs
  • Scale resources as needed
  • Convert fixed costs to variable
Slide 11 / 20

Benefit from Massive Economies of Scale

By using cloud computing, you can achieve a lower variable cost than you can get on your own. Because usage from hundreds of thousands of customers is aggregated in the cloud, providers can achieve higher economies of scale.

📈

Lower prices translate to better value for your business

Slide 12 / 20

Stop Guessing About Capacity

Eliminate guessing on your infrastructure capacity needs. When you make a capacity decision prior to deploying an application, you often end up either sitting on expensive idle resources or dealing with limited capacity.

❌ Old Way

Guess capacity needs weeks or months in advance

✅ Cloud Way

Scale up or down automatically based on actual demand

Slide 13 / 20

Increase Speed and Agility

In a cloud computing environment, new IT resources are only a click away, which means you reduce the time to make those resources available to your developers from weeks to just minutes.

Results in:

  • Dramatic increase in agility for the organisation
  • Lower cost and time to experiment and develop
  • Faster innovation and time to market
  • Rapid prototyping and testing
Slide 14 / 20

Stop Spending Money Running and Maintaining Data Centres

Focus on projects that differentiate your business, not the infrastructure. Cloud computing lets you focus on your own customers, rather than on the heavy lifting of racking, stacking, and powering servers.

❌ Undifferentiated Heavy Lifting

  • Data centre operations
  • Hardware maintenance
  • Capacity planning
  • Security management

✅ Focus on Your Business

  • Customer innovation
  • Product development
  • Business strategy
  • Market differentiation
Slide 15 / 20

Go Global in Minutes

Easily deploy your application in multiple regions around the world with just a few clicks. This means you can provide lower latency and a better experience for your customers at minimal cost.

🌐

Deploy globally, act locally

What is Amazon Web Services (AWS)?

AWS is a secure cloud platform that offers a broad set of global cloud-based products called services that are designed to work together.

Key Facts

  • Launched in 2006
  • Offers over 200+ fully featured services
  • Available in multiple geographic regions worldwide
  • Pay-as-you-go pricing model
  • Used by millions of customers globally
