5. These spec of systems for 4K video editing are not Good at all, First The CPU:
An Intel core i5 4 core processor is not good enough for 4K video Editing. This is Becasue it lacks the power needed to handle the task. Only having 4 cores means the CPU cannot Decode and Execute enough instructions to handle the Timelines efficiently. If you upgraded the CPUs to intel core i9 14900Ks you would have 24 cores and 32 threads. This would be more than enough to handle the task. Or you can buy a mac studio which would be more powerful than a desktop computer as they are more optimised for the task.
The RAM, Only having 8GB of RAM means that the Video project File won't be able to be opened and edited nicely. You want 32GB+ of RAM for smooth editing. This will allow the video project file to be opened and edited smoothly without any lag or stuttering. You would want the RAM to be clocked at 3200MHz or higher for the best performance.
Using Integrated Graphics is not good as they share resources with the CPU, This includes RAM, So you were working with 4GB of RAM before Using Integrated Graphics. You should upgrade to a dedicated graphics card with at least 8GB of VRAM to make 4K video editing smooth. Upgrading to a 4070 or above will be more than enough for 4K video editing. having a Dedicated GPU will also allow you to use the GPU for Rendering making the render times much faster taking an 8 Hour Render for 10 minutes to a 1 minute render for 10 minutes. This will also improve the quality of the Final Render.
Lastly 1TB HDDs for storage is HORRIBLE for 4K video editing, they are Slow and will take ages to load Massive 4K files off of. You should upgrade to an SSD with at least 2TB of storage for smooth editing. This will allow you to load and save files quickly without any lag or stuttering. You could also set up a NAS on your network to store your files on a network drive. This will allow you to store final renders and unedited footage on it. This will save space on your Local drives and allow the files on the NAS to be Compressed with Lossless Compression to save space on the NAS. Also Using a HDD NAS with a SSD Cach will allow for faster Upload and Download speeds to and from the NAS as you won't be writing directly to the HDDs. You should also consider using a RAID array for even better performance.
