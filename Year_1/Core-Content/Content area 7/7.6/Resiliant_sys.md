The company could set up a warm or hot standby
A hot standby would share the load until a server dies then one would take over for the meantime. 
A warm standby is where teh backup is running but not serving traffic until needed, this lowers cost but means that the failover time is slightly longer
A cold standby would mean that the backup is compleatley off until it is needed meaning the failover is long but it costs the least amount of money.

Another thing the company could do is, hace network redundancy like having a dual ISP connection (2 compleatley different ISPs), Have multiple switches and routers
This costs money but ensures minimal down time while people are working.
