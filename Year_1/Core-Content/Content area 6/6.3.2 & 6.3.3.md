## Key Concepts to Remember

### Structured Data
- Organised data with predefined schema
- Fits into rows and columns
- Easy to search, query, and analyse
- Stored in databases and spreadsheets
- Examples: numbers, dates, fixed categories

### Unstructured Data
- No predefined format or schema
- Free-form content with variable structure
- Difficult to search without AI
- Stored as files, documents, media
- Examples: text, images, videos, audio

### Important Relationships
- **Quantitative = Structured:** Numerical data that can be measured and counted. Always structured because numbers follow consistent formats. Can perform mathematical operations and statistical analysis.
- **Qualitative = Unstructured:** Descriptive data capturing qualities and characteristics. Always unstructured because opinions and descriptions don't fit predefined formats. Requires interpretation and context.
