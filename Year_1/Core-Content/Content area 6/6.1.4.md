# 6.1.4 How Organisations Use Data - Exam Questions

### Question 1 (6 marks)

**Scenario:** An online streaming service notices that their servers experience extremely high load between 7-10 PM every evening, causing buffering and slow performance for users.

**Question:** Explain how the streaming service could use system performance analysis to address this problem. In your answer, describe:
* What data they should analyse (2 marks)
* What solutions they could implement based on this analysis (4 marks)

**1. Data Analysis**
To address the buffering issues, the streaming service needs to analyse specific metrics during the 7-10 PM window. They should look at **Load metrics**, specifically CPU usage and Memory utilisation, to see if the hardware is maxing out. They should also analyse **Throughput data**, such as the number of concurrent users and the network bandwidth being used, to identify if the bottleneck is due to too many people trying to watch at once.

**2. Solutions**
Based on this analysis, the service could implement **Auto-scaling**. This involves setting up the system to automatically add more servers or resources during the peak hours of 7-10 PM and then scale them back down when demand drops, ensuring users get smooth playback without the company paying for unused servers all day.
Another solution is to use **Load Balancing**. This distributes the incoming user traffic evenly across multiple servers so that no single server becomes overwhelmed and crashes. By spreading the load, the service ensures that even if one server is busy, others can pick up the slack, preventing buffering and improving the overall user experience.

### Question 2 (8 marks)

**Scenario:** A supermarket chain analyses customer transaction data and identifies that 60% of customers who buy nappies also purchase baby food, but only 15% of baby food buyers purchase nappies.

**Question:** Evaluate how the supermarket could use this pattern analysis for targeted marketing. In your answer, discuss:
* Two different marketing strategies based on this data (4 marks)
* The benefits of targeted marketing compared to sending the same offer to all customers (4 marks)

**1. Marketing Strategies**
* **Cross-Selling:** Since the data shows a strong link between nappies and baby food, the supermarket can use **Cross-Selling** techniques. They could print a coupon for "20% off baby food" that triggers specifically when a customer scans a pack of nappies at the till. This encourages the customer to buy the complementary item immediately.
* **Targeted Loyalty Offers:** The supermarket can use **Customer Segmentation** to identify the 15% of baby food buyers who *don't* buy nappies. These might be customers who buy nappies elsewhere. The supermarket could send them a targeted email or app notification with a strong discount on the supermarket's own brand of nappies to try and win that portion of their business.

**2. Benefits of Targeted Marketing**
The main benefit of targeted marketing is **Cost Effectiveness**. Sending a generic leaflet to every customer is expensive and wasteful because people without children will just throw it away. By using data to target only parents, the supermarket saves money on printing and postage.
Another benefit is **Higher Conversion Rates**. Relevant offers are much more likely to be used. If a customer receives a discount for a product they actually need (like nappies), they are more likely to visit the store and spend money, whereas irrelevant offers can annoy customers and lead to them unsubscribing from marketing emails.

### Question 3 (6 marks)

**Scenario:** A company's IT department monitors user login activity and notices an employee's account logged in from London at 9:00 AM, then from New York at 9:30 AM the same day.

**Question:** Explain why this user monitoring data indicates a security threat and describe two actions the company should take in response.

**1. Why this is a threat**
This data indicates a security threat because of the **Impossible Travel** scenario. It is physically impossible for a person to travel from London to New York in 30 minutes. This suggests that the employee's credentials (username and password) have been stolen or compromised and are being used by an unauthorised person in a different location.

**2. Actions to take**
* **Lock the Account:** The IT department should immediately **Lock or Suspend** the account. This stops the attacker from accessing any more files or doing further damage while the investigation takes place. This acts as immediate damage control.
* **Force Password Reset:** Once the immediate threat is contained, the company must force a **Password Reset** for that user. They should also enable or enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) to ensure that even if the attacker has the password, they cannot log in again without the second form of verification.
