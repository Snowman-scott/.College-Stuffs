What is the CIA Triad?

Foundation of Information Security

The CIA Triad is a model widely used in information security to guide policies and practices for protecting sensitive data.

It represents the three core principles that must be maintained to ensure data security in any organisation.

🔒

Confidentiality

Protecting information from unauthorised access

✓

Integrity

Maintaining accuracy and completeness of data

🔄

Availability

Ensuring data is accessible when needed

Slide 2 / 8

The Three Pillars

🔒 Confidentiality

Ensuring that information is accessible only to those authorised to access it, thereby protecting sensitive information from unauthorised disclosure.

Techniques include: Encryption, access controls, and security protocols.

✓ Integrity

Maintaining and assuring the accuracy and completeness of data. This means ensuring that information is not altered in unauthorised ways.

Tools include: Checksums, access controls, and audit trails.

🔄 Availability

Making sure that data and resources are available to authorised users when needed. This involves keeping systems running and accessible.

Measures include: Hardware maintenance, network bandwidth management, and disaster recovery plans.

Slide 3 / 8

Confidentiality

Protecting Sensitive Information

Confidentiality is about protecting sensitive information from unauthorised access and exposure. It involves a range of practices and controls.

Key Methods:

🔐 Encryption

The process of encoding data so that only authorised parties can decode and read it. Can be applied to data at rest, in transit, and in use.

👤 Access Control

Defining who has authority to access certain data. Includes authentication (passwords, biometrics) and authorisation.

📊 Data Classification

Classifying data based on sensitivity (public, internal, confidential, secret) to apply appropriate controls.

🏢 Physical Security

Securing physical locations like data centres against unauthorised access, theft, or damage.

📚 Policies and Training

Educating employees about the importance of confidentiality and having clear policies on data handling and sharing.

Slide 4 / 8

Integrity

Maintaining Data Accuracy

Integrity ensures that information remains accurate, consistent, and trustworthy over its entire lifecycle.

Key Methods:

✓ Data Validation

Techniques like input validation ensure data entered into systems is correct. Error checking mechanisms like checksums and hashes detect alterations.

📝 Version Control

Crucial for maintaining integrity in environments where multiple revisions are common, such as software development.

📋 Audit Trails

Keeping detailed logs of who accessed what data and when, along with what changes were made. Crucial for tracing integrity issues.

💾 Data Backup

Regular backups and redundant systems prevent data loss and ensure a consistent, accurate version is always available.

Slide 5 / 8

Availability

Ensuring Accessible Systems

Availability ensures that information and resources are accessible to authorised users when needed.

Key Methods:

🔧 Hardware Maintenance

Regularly maintaining and updating hardware to prevent failures and ensure continuous operation.

🌐 Network Management

Ensuring reliable network infrastructure with minimal downtime, implementing load balancing to distribute traffic evenly.

🆘 Disaster Recovery

Developing comprehensive plans for data recovery and system restoration in the event of disasters like fires, floods, or cyber attacks.

🔄 Redundant Systems

Implementing redundant systems (multiple power sources, servers) and failover capabilities to switch to backups during failures.

🔄 Regular Updates

Keeping software and operating systems updated to fix vulnerabilities and improve performance.

Slide 6 / 8

Working Together

The Triad in Action

Each component of the CIA Triad plays a critical role in the overall security posture of an organisation.

  • Interconnected Protection: They work in tandem to protect against a wide array of threats, ranging from cyber attacks and internal breaches to physical security threats and natural disasters.
  • Balance is Key: Sometimes there are trade-offs between the three principles. For example, increasing availability might slightly reduce security, requiring careful balance.
  • Comprehensive Approach: Implementing robust measures across all three areas is essential for safeguarding data integrity and ensuring the trustworthiness and resilience of information systems.
  • Risk Management: Understanding the CIA Triad helps organisations identify vulnerabilities and implement appropriate security controls.

✅ Best Practice

A strong security posture requires continuous monitoring and improvement of all three CIA components, not just focusing on one area.
