2. Scenario: TechStartup Ltd is a small software development company with 35 employees in Manchester. They create mobile apps for local businesses and charge for development and maintenance.

Question: Identify and explain the sector, size category, and business model. Justify your answer with reference to the scenario. [8 Marks]


They are in the technology sector, classified as a small business, and operate in the software development industry. Their business model is subscription-based, where clients pay a monthly fee for app development and maintenance services. This model allows TechStartup Ltd to provide continuous support and updates to their clients, ensuring their apps remain up-to-date and functional. Additionally, the subscription model enables TechStartup Ltd to generate recurring revenue and predict future income, which is beneficial for financial planning and investment decisions. Furthermore, the subscription model allows TechStartup Ltd to offer flexible pricing options and tailor their services to meet the needs of different clients, which can lead to increased customer satisfaction and loyalty.

3. Scenario: The NHS is launching a digital health platform for patients to book appointments and access records. HealthTech Solutions (private sector) is contracted to develop it.

Question: Compare the sectors and business models of NHS and HealthTech Solutions. Explain how their different characteristics might affect their approach to this project.
[10 marks]

The NHS is a public sector organization that operates in the healthcare industry. Their business model is based on providing free or low-cost healthcare services to the population. This model allows the NHS to prioritize patient care and accessibility over profit maximization. Additionally, the NHS operates under strict regulations and guidelines set by the government, which can impact their approach to the project.

HealthTech Solutions, on the other hand, is a private sector organization that operates in the technology industry. Their business model is based on providing software development and maintenance services to clients. This model allows HealthTech Solutions to generate revenue through contract work and project-based fees. Additionally, HealthTech Solutions operates under market forces and customer demand, which can impact their approach to the project.

The different characteristics of the NHS and HealthTech Solutions can affect their approach to the project in several ways. For example, the NHS may prioritize patient safety and accessibility over cost-effectiveness, while HealthTech Solutions may prioritize cost-effectiveness over patient safety. Additionally, the NHS may have more resources and funding available for the project, while HealthTech Solutions may have more flexibility in terms of project scope and timeline. Finally, the NHS may have more regulatory and compliance requirements to consider, while HealthTech Solutions may have more freedom to innovate and experiment with new technologies.
