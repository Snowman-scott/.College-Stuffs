Phishing – phishing is where they mass send out emails or scam messages to people to try get them to open a link or send them money so they can install malware on your device or So they can steal your money, This can be mitigated via checking everything to check for spelling, punctuation and grammar issues as well as the senders email and how they address you

Spear phishing – More targeted Phishing where they research about people you work with, your job, what sector your in and find ways you can be manipulated to get money or account access

Pretexting – The Scammer invents a (pretext) to engage the victim in targeted scenario to manipulate them more, this will make the story more believable and make them more likely to give over information, money or get passwords reset

Baiting – The act of Human nature, Dropping a USB on the Ground knowing someone would be likely to plug it in, When they plug it in It offloads malware onto the machine which they can use to gain access to the computer or the Network which can be infiltrated and have key loggers and more put onto it so they can see exactly what you’re doing.

Tailgating – Also known as piggybacking, using someone else’s login or access cards/ fobs to gain access to the building or location to try and gain access to networking equipment or do other Malicious practices. This is harder to do when the building has cameras everywhere and you would get arrested or shouted at for not scanning in one at a time as in lots of places it’s their way of keeping track of who is and is not in the building.
